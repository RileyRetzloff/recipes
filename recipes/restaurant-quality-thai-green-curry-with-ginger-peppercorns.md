### Ingredients

- 1 can (13.5-14 oz) full-fat coconut milk (cream separated from liquid)  
- 3-4 tbsp canned green curry paste  
- 2-3 cloves garlic, minced  
- 1 tbsp ginger and/or white peppercorns, finely grated  
- 1 tbsp lemongrass, finely grated (using a Microplane)  
- 2tbsp dried fake chicken bullion  
- 4-6 Kaffir lime leaves, torn  
- 1 tbsp palm sugar (or brown sugar)  
- extra firm tofu pressed and coated in corn starch and air fryer till crispy  
- Vegetables: 1/4" sliced carrots, 1/2" sliced Chinese eggplant, green beans, broccoli, 1/3" sliced zucchini  
- Fresh Thai basil and lime juice to finish  
  
### Instructions

1. Simmer the thick coconut cream at the top of the can until the oil separates.  
2. Add the curry paste, garlic, ginger and/or white peppercorns, and lemongrass. Fry for 2-3 minutes until fragrant.  
3. Add the chicken bullion, remaining thin coconut milk, torn lime leaves, and palm sugar.  
4. Simmer the sauce uncovered for 5-10 minutes until it reaches your desired thickness.  
5. Add the carrots and simmer for 5 minutes.  
6. Add the eggplant, green beans, and broccoli, and simmer for 5 minutes.  
7. Add the zucchini and tofu and simmer for a final 3-4 minutes.  
8. Remove from heat and stir in fresh Thai basil and a squeeze of lime juice.  
  
  
Note: If using both ginger and white peppercorns to substitute for galangal, use 1 tbsp finely grated fresh ginger and 1/4 tsp ground white peppercorns. Optionally, add a pinch of lime zest or a squeeze of lime juice to mimic galangal's citrus notes.
