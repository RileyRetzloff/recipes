---
title: berry walnut smoothie
author: Riley Retzloff
created: 2026-11-06
updated: 2026-11-06
---

- 150g mixed berries (i use Kirkland -- blueberries, raspberries, and blackberries)
- 60g walnuts
- 150g plain whole yogurt
- 15g maple syrup
- 0.5s squirt liquid splenda
- pinch of salt
- a little water, probably ~120g

blend in vitamix. work up to highest speed and run for 30s.
