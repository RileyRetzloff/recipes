# Kale, Italian Sausage, & White Bean Stew (Slop)

### Notes
- **Servings:** Makes ~8 large servings
- **Equipment:** This can all be done in one pot (Instant Pot recommended).
- **Tips:** 
  - Be liberal with Italian seasoning (and salt if you know how to gauge it).
  - Be conservative with vinegar and lemon juice.
  - If you really screw up, you can add potatoes as a hail mary—they can soak up your #!$%-ups.

---

### Ingredients

#### Base Ingredients
- **2–4 tbsp** avocado or olive oil *(for sautéing)*
- **680–900g (24–32 oz)** hot Italian sausage, sliced
- **1 medium/large** yellow onion, diced
- **5-ish cloves** garlic *(or more, if you're into that)*
- **250g** carrots, chopped
- **250g** celery hearts, chopped
- **350g** red potatoes (or other variety), cubed
- **285g (10 oz)** Tuscan kale (or other variety)
- **425g (15 oz)** can cannellini beans
- **1,180ml (~5 cups)** vegetable or beef broth

#### Seasonings & Adjustments
- Salt, to taste
- **2–3 tbsp** Italian seasoning, to taste
- **2–4** bay leaves
- **1 tsp** cumin, to taste
- **3 tbsp** balsamic vinegar, to taste
- **1–3 tbsp** lemon juice, to taste
- **2–4 tbsp** Worcestershire, to taste *(optional)*
- Black pepper, to taste *(optional)*
- Hot red pepper flakes, to taste *(optional)*
- Sriracha, to taste *(optional)*

---

### Preparation

1. **Prep & Initial Sauté:**  
   If Italian sausage is raw, sauté until firm enough to slice, then slice and set aside (I put it with all the cut veggies in one big bowl). Slice all your veggies now or during the next step.

2. **Build the Soup:**  
   Sauté onion until soft; add garlic and desired dry spices/seasonings, stirring at least until fragrant. Add in carrots, potatoes, celery, Italian sausage, and broth, and bring to a boil *(use the soup function on IP)*.

3. **Incorporate & Start Slow Cook:**  
   Turn off heat and incorporate kale and balsamic vinegar. Slow cook for 2 hours, but set a timer for 30 minutes or so to check on the flavor. *(If using IP, ensure lid is set to vent).*

4. **Flavor Cultivation:**  
   After 30 minutes-ish, check the flavor of the broth. Salt is your best friend; it brings out all the other flavors in your soup along with it. Try adding more salt before more seasonings. Adding seasonings one at a time is your other best friend. You can always mix and taste, mix and taste. Nurture and cultivate this soup like a fine garden. It’s hard to f*** it up, but if you do, there’s no going back.
   - **Not salty enough?** You’re probably right.
   - **Not earthy or musky enough?** Add Italian seasoning, bay leaves, or Worcestershire.
   - **Not tangy enough?** Add lemon juice for bright tang or balsamic for dark tang.
   - **Not savory enough?** Try a teaspoon of cumin or maybe even some more broth base if it’s not salty enough as well.
   - **Not spicy enough?** Hot red pepper flakes or sriracha.

5. **Finish:**  
   Once done cultivating your desired flavor profile with seasonings, spices, sauces, and juices, let the slow cook cycle finish.

6. **Serve:**  
   Taste it, make any final alterations, and enjoy!
